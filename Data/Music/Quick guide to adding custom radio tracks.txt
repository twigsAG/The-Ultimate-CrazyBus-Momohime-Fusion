To add any custom songs to the Radio,
you will have to put an .ogg or .mp3 file of your choice and rename it 'CustomSong#.ogg' 
('#' is a placeholder for an actual number)
Up to 15 songs can be added to the in-game radio. 
If the music you want isn't an .ogg or an .mp3 file than you will have to convert it as such.

NOTICE ABOUT SIMILAR AND/OR CONFLICTING FILENAMES: 
You CAN'T have two songs named 'CustomSong#' in both supported formats, NOR can you mix and match .ogg and .mp3 files.
This isn't supported due to technical limitations.
Plus, make sure there aren't two identically named songs in each format.
